## 1. The Five Euclid Lenses

### 1.1 Predictive Lens (What does the theory forecast?)
Forecasts endless emergence of primes without upper bound; predicts constructibility of regular n-gons via prime factorization.

### 1.2 Causal Lens (What invariant structures force the result?)
Driven by the divisibility ordering of natural numbers and the well-ordering principle of Z_+.

### 1.3 Counterfactual Lens (What if a key hypothesis is dropped?)
If the unit magnitude is infinitesimal or non-Archimedean, the Euclidean algorithm fails to terminate in finite steps.

### 1.4 Topological Lens (What is the space/bundle/cohomology structure?)
Discrete affine 1-manifold Z embedded in Archimedean line R; trivial discrete topology on Z.

### 1.5 Geometric Lens (What shape, lattice, or metric invariants govern?)
Flat Euclidean metric with zero curvature; pythagorean distance metric on planar triangles.
