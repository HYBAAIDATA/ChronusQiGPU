## 2. Precision definition (what folding is)

### 2.1 Industry family (category context — EXTERNAL with citation)

In the broader market, “data / model folding” usually means **tensor-network compression**: reshape a high-dimensional array or neural weight tensor into a chain/grid of smaller tensors (e.g. **MPS**, **MPO**, **tensor train**), keep dominant correlations, discard redundant structure, reassemble (unfold) for use.

- **Quantum-inspired, not quantum-dependent** — algebra from many-body physics; execution on classical CPUs/GPUs today.
- Selling point: ROI (memory, inference, edge) without requiring the buyer to believe in near-term quantum hardware.

**Reference competitor / category proof (not HYBA results):** Multiverse Computing **CompactifAI** (quantum-inspired tensor networks / MPO on LLM weights).

| Llama 3.1-8B / 3.3-70B compressed offerings; third-party energy/accuracy studies (e.g. Sopra Steria arXiv:2507.08836); vendor announcements ~80% size / efficiency claims | Vendor PR + independent eval papers | Market readiness of TN-LLM compression; **not** HYBA seal |

**Adjacent, separate technology family (do not lump into HYBA fold GTM):** DNA storage, holographic/optical storage — research-real, not the same product motion as tensor-network or φ-fold compression.
