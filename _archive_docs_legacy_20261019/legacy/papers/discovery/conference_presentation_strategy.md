## Presentation Materials

### Standard Presentation Template

**Structure**:

1. Motivation and Background (5 minutes)
2. Main Results (10 minutes)
3. Key Proofs (10 minutes)
4. Applications and Implications (5 minutes)
5. Open Problems (5 minutes)
6. Q&A (remaining time)

### Visual Elements

- **High-quality mathematical notation**: LaTeX-rendered equations
- **Geometric visualizations**: 3D renderings of M_32 manifold
- **Algorithm animations**: Step-by-step algorithm demonstrations
- **Numerical results**: Plots and tables showing convergence
- **Comparison charts**: MQP vs P vs BQP complexity

### Demo Materials

- **Live code demonstrations**: Running MQP algorithms
- **Interactive visualizations**: Manifold exploration tools
- **Numerical experiments**: Real-time theorem verification
- **Comparison benchmarks**: Classical vs quantum vs mathematical quantum

## Risk Mitigation

### Conference Rejection

- **Risk**: Papers may be rejected from top conferences
- **Mitigation**: Submit to multiple venues, have backup conferences ready

### Presentation Quality

- **Risk**: Poor presentation may damage reputation
- **Mitigation**: Practice extensively, get feedback from collaborators

### Networking Challenges

- **Risk**: Difficulty establishing connections with senior researchers
- **Mitigation**: Start with junior researchers, build up to senior contacts

### Time Constraints

- **Risk**: Too many conferences may dilute focus
- **Mitigation**: Prioritize high-impact venues, limit to 3-4 major conferences per year
