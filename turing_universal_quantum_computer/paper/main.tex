\begin{abstract}
This working draft documents the organization of TURING's exact execution
software, typed public interfaces and reproducibility surfaces. The accompanying
package-validation experiments check exact observables and a finite variational
family. They are not evidence of a new complexity advantage, a general continuous
variational solver, or an implemented correction/control extension. A complete
research contribution and evaluation must be specified before submission.

\section{Experimental methodology}
The package-validation cases are a Bell-state Z expectation, a radical-valued
single-qubit expectation, and minimization over three explicitly declared exact
states. Configurations, manifests and table-generation scripts are separate from
implementation code. These cases establish software invariants, not comparative
performance. A broader evaluation requires independently selected baselines and
resource/uncertainty definitions.
