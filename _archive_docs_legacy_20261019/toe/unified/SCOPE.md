# Scope

Supported:

- Import `toe.unified.UnifiedAnalyticsPlatform` inside a Python process.
- Catalog of 1063 connector definitions across the 19 classes in `DISPLACED_CLASSES`.
- Protocols `jdbc` (SQLite), `rest`, `odata`, `kafka`, `sdk`, and `opcua`, each with a fixture driver.
- Ingest, dedup, entity index, zlib storage, quality policy, quarantine, release, audit list, and JSON export/import.
- `exact_linear` train, retrain, rollback, column transfer, and a live pointer swap.
- Health loop, drift retrain, selectivity plans, and the consciousness threshold.
- The question grammar and use-ordered widgets.
- Industry functions, including fraud lift and sensor batches.
- The cost formula, the 23-system case, the reference workloads, and the three-university ledger with outcome counts.
- One role table, the risk register, and the patent register.
- Phase gates at 50 connectors, 500 connectors, satisfaction 0.95, processing reduction 0.90, three-year ROI 4.0, and 10 patents.

Not a deployment:

- No compose service runs this package. `platform.start()` does not open a port.
- Real rows used by the coverage layers are the vendored UCI Iris file and the program cost sheet, each with a SHA-256 in `tests/fixtures/toe_unified/checksums.json`.
- `make verify-unified` runs invariant, adversarial, property, unit, integration, end-to-end, then benchmark.
- A catalog row is not a connection to that vendor.
- SHA256d share validation is outside this package.
- Reference workload figures are the step counts in `productivity.REFERENCE`. Live samples are separate.
- The health loop runs only after `monitor.start()`.
