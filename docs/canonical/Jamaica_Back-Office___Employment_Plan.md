## BLUF

HYBA Data is headquartered in the Dubai International Financial Centre (DIFC) with an Innovations Licence, and the Founder is based in London. However, as a Jamaican-led company, the absolute operational mandate is to offshore every possible back-office, administrative, and technical support function to Kingston, Jamaica. We are building the computational capital of the Caribbean by creating high-value, tech-native employment.

---

## 1. Operational Functions Destined for Jamaica

We will not outsource to traditional BPO hubs (India, Philippines). Jamaica will be the operational engine room for HYBA Data's global CIaaS delivery. 

### A. CIaaS Telemetry & Billing Operations

- **Function:** Monitoring the `savings_metering.py` dashboards for global enterprise clients.
- **Roles Created:** Telemetry Analysts, Cloud Billing Specialists, Data Auditors.
- **Impact:** Jamaicans will be managing the mathematical billing infrastructure for global Fortune 500 companies.

### B. SDK Level 1 & Level 2 Support

- **Function:** Providing frontline integration support for enterprise developers using the HYBA Data SDK.
- **Roles Created:** Technical Support Engineers, SDK Integration Specialists, Developer Advocates.
- **Impact:** Upskilling local tech talent to support an advanced quantum-mathematical software stack.

### C. Foundation & Administrative Execution

- **Function:** Running the HYBA Foundation, managing the Turing Scholarships, and handling global corporate administration.
- **Roles Created:** Program Managers, HR, Finance Operations, Executive Assistants.

## 2. The Execution Strategy

1. **Immediate Setup:** Establish a secure, high-bandwidth operational hub in Kingston.
2. **Local Hiring Initiative:** Partner with the University of the West Indies (UWI) and local tech incubators to source and train the first cohort of Telemetry Analysts and SDK Support Engineers.
3. **The COBBLA Multiplier:** Use the COBBLA free IDE initiative to identify top-tier self-taught Jamaican developers and recruit them directly into the Level 2 support and operations teams.

*We are transforming Jamaican talent from consumers of foreign technology into operators of global infrastructure.*
