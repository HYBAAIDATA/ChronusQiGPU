## 1. BLUF

Industry sequencing is sound: **cash and trust from near-term billable work first; long-horizon science second.**

For HYBA, map that logic onto **two primary revenue engines**, then gated premiums:

1. **HYBA Data fold wedge (HERMES / PULVINI / PHI-1)** — classical-hardware ROI, CIaaS gain-share, mass enterprise FinOps. **Lead here for Data GTM.**
2. **PQC migration & crypto-agility (AL HARAS security lane)** — compliance deadline + budget line in gov/finance/health. **Lead here for security/sovereign GTM.**

Then: **optimisation as embedded feature**, **QKD as narrow managed link**, **simulation as co-dev**, **QML as paid exploration only**.

**Strategic cash-flow rule:** Fund simulation/QML ambition with fold + PQC revenue — do not sell them as mature platforms today.

- May sell **standards-aligned PQC migration** and **inventory honesty**.
- May pair with topological / fail-closed governance **only with evidence tier** from forensic/capability reviews — not “mathematically impossible to hack.”

- HYBA Data fold and AL HARAS security remain **separate commercial faces** that can attach on one MSA with different schedules.

Government agencies, banks, healthcare systems, defence contractors, telecoms — compliance-forced first.

## 5. Optimisation (annealing / QAOA-class) — feature, not platform

**Nobody buys “quantum optimisation” as a standalone platform in honest GTM.**

Embed as premium tier inside a vertical outcome:

- Supply chain / routing → accelerated route optimisation add-on
- Energy → grid load / renewable dispatch optimisation
- Finance → portfolio risk / rebalancing optimisation

**Honesty rule:** sell **incremental improvement on narrow problem classes** where classical baselines are published in the pilot. Overselling this segment is where quantum startups lose credibility.

**HYBA mapping:** not a cold-open Data SKU; optional feature after fold trust or inside a vertical schedule — requires CPO elevation + matched benchmark protocol.

---

## 6. Simulation — co-development, not shelfware

Highest long-term value; least productized.

- **Package as:** joint R&D, simulation credits, national-lab style partnerships.
- **Buyers:** pharma, chemicals, battery/materials, government labs.
- **Not:** self-serve subscription that implies production drug/materials discovery out of the box.

HYBA health/protein narratives stay **RESTRICTED / INTERNAL** until biomedical ethics, methods, and independent validation exist (founder continuity safeguards).

---

## 7. QML — innovation lab only

Given thin real advantage vs classical ML today:

- **Sell:** paid pilot — “test whether a quantum-inspired approach helps *this* dataset.”
- **Do not sell:** production replacement for classical ML pipelines.
- Credibility risk if customer benchmarks and finds no edge — price exploration explicitly and document negative results (posterity mandate).
