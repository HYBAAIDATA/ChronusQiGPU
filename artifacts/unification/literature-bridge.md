# Literature bridge — research hypothesis

These papers illustrate related tensor techniques. Whether they converge on the proposed exact-field/topological principle is an unproven hypothesis; the papers do not establish it. The selection is not an exhaustive citation-network review.

## Continuous tensor abstraction

The authors describe a programming abstraction with real-valued indices. [continuous](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.01742)

## Machine learning invariants of tensors

The authors enumerate tensor contractions and report five invariants in their six-dimensional three-form case. [invariants](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.23750)

## Tensor-network fluid simulation

The authors study tensor-network methods for two-dimensional turbulence; this is not a Navier–Stokes regularity proof. [fluids](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.17823)

## Tensorizing neural networks

The authors investigate tensor-train parametrization of neural-network layers; reductions are workload-dependent. [compression](https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.06569)

## Quantum circuit simulation

The authors study classical simulation of quantum circuits using tensor-network contraction and graph treewidth. [quantum](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0511069)

## Limits

No Millennium Problem solution, quantum advantage, universal compression, clinical outcome or security assurance is claimed.
