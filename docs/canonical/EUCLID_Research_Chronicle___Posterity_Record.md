## Ineffable comparison boundary
Public reporting describes Ineffable Intelligence as pursuing a reinforcement-learning “superlearner” that learns from environmental experience without human demonstrations. EUCLID now has a controlled experience-learning implementation, but no direct Ineffable system, code, weights, evaluation endpoint, or matched protocol was available. Therefore this archive makes no claim that EUCLID has outperformed Ineffable. “Make Ineffable look like an abacus” is recorded as a falsifiable benchmark objective: superiority must be earned through matched, external, reproducible evidence.

## Scientific boundary
Architecture is not capability. Internal benchmarks are not independent validation. Memory integrity is not cognition. Emergence curves are not proof of emergence. AGI and ASI gates remain false. The preserved architecture establishes a testable substrate and an auditable research programme.
