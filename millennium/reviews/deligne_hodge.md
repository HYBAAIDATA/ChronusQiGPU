## 1. The Five Euclid Lenses

### 1.1 Predictive Lens (What does the theory forecast?)
Predicts weight and Hodge filtrations for arbitrary singular or quasi-projective algebraic varieties.

### 1.2 Causal Lens (What invariant structures force the result?)
Action of Frobenius on l-adic cohomology and strict degeneration of the Hodge-to-de Rham spectral sequence at E_1.

### 1.3 Counterfactual Lens (What if a key hypothesis is dropped?)
Dropping algebraic structure (considering general smooth manifolds with boundary) destroys the purity of graded pieces Gr_k^W.

### 1.4 Topological Lens (What is the space/bundle/cohomology structure?)
Simplicial resolutions of singular varieties; tubular neighborhoods and compactifications with normal crossings divisors.

### 1.5 Geometric Lens (What shape, lattice, or metric invariants govern?)
Degenerating families of Kähler structures; nilpotent orbits and Hodge metric singularities.

## 2. Key Claims & Status Breakdown

| Claim / Theorem | Page / Index | Status | Formulation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Deligne Mixed Hodge Theorem | Deligne Mixed Hodge Theorem | **PROVEN** | The cohomology of any complex algebraic variety (possibly singular or non-compact) canonically carries a mixed Hodge structure. |
| Strict Positivity & Degeneration | Strict Positivity & Degeneration | **PROVEN** | The Hodge-de Rham spectral sequence degenerates at E_1 for smooth projective varieties over C. |
| Deligne's Theorem on Absolute Hodge Cycles | Deligne's Theorem on Absolute Hodge Cycles | **PROVEN** | Every Hodge cycle on an abelian variety of CM-type is an absolute Hodge cycle. |
| Hodge Conjecture on Abelian Varieties | Hodge Conjecture on Abelian Varieties | **OPEN** | Hodge classes on abelian varieties are generated by divisor classes and algebraic correspondences. |
