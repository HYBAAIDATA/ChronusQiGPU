# Statement on Millennium / Clay Problems

PhiEnterprise includes research tooling (the "Millennium suite") and numerical experiments that explore algebraic and spectral phenomena (e.g., the M₃₂ manifold, Yang–Mills finite-group mass gap on 2I, and related spectral geometry). These are research outputs and computational artifacts, not mathematical proofs of Millennium Prize problems.

Important points:

- The repository explicitly marks claims and evidence classes. See `reports/` and `millennium/` modules for testable evidence and open items (e.g., `millennium/clay_checklist.py`).
- Where the code computes finite-lattice spectra, it reports measured results and flags any extrapolation to continuum limits as "partial" or "modeled".
- The project does not assert a new proof of any Clay Millennium Prize problem. Any suggestion to the contrary must be accompanied by a formal proof submitted to appropriate mathematical venues and peer review.

If you wish to publish or claim novelty in that direction, we should:
1. Prepare a clear separation of research artifacts from product artifacts.
2. Prepare formal write-ups suitable for academic submission (LaTeX + reproducible code package).
3. Engage independent mathematical reviewers before public claims.

If you want, I can draft a short research disclosure or an academic preprint skeleton (title, abstract, background, computations, limitations) that you can iterate with domain mathematicians.
