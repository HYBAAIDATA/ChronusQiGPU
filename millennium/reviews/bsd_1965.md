## 1. The Five Euclid Lenses

### 1.1 Predictive Lens (What does the theory forecast?)
Predicts that the analytic vanishing order ord_{s=1} L(E, s) matches the algebraic rank r = dim E(Q) otimes Q.

### 1.2 Causal Lens (What invariant structures force the result?)
Euler product density prod N_p / p accumulates point abundance across finite fields, reflecting global rational solutions.

### 1.3 Counterfactual Lens (What if a key hypothesis is dropped?)
If Sha(E) were infinite, the leading coefficient quotient would diverge or collapse to zero.

### 1.4 Topological Lens (What is the space/bundle/cohomology structure?)
The 1-dimensional complex torus C / Lambda isomorphic to the Riemann surface of the elliptic curve E(C).

### 1.5 Geometric Lens (What shape, lattice, or metric invariants govern?)
Real period lattice Omega_E = int_{E(R)} dx/(2y) and Néron-Tate canonical height quadratic form h_hat on E(Q).
