## Owned authority

Ingest health/capability evidence; preserve unknown/unavailable states; quarantine unhealthy capabilities; apply circuit-breaker policy; stage bounded recovery DAGs; manage tenant admission/isolation, qualification refresh and rollback through registered authorised handlers. Learning cannot change correctness, conformance, storage integrity or safety policy.

No arbitrary command, SQL, filesystem handler, dynamic execution or runtime source rewriting. No second proof, pool, execution-router, storage or policy root.

## Non-mining acceptance requirement

Inject stale/unknown enterprise service health, a quarantined capability and an unauthorised recovery request; confirm honest status, bounded authorised actions, no mutation from telemetry alone, restart truth reacquisition and auditable rollback. No fresh run is asserted.

## Customer schedule

Define managed resources, authorised actions, approvals, tenancy, evidence retention and exclusions. No unbounded self-healing, guaranteed zero downtime or global failover claim.
