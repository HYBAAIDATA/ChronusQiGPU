## 1. The Five Euclid Lenses

### 1.1 Predictive Lens (What does the theory forecast?)
Forecasts dimensions of Chow groups CH^k(X) and intermediate Jacobians J^k(X) under algebraic equivalence.

### 1.2 Causal Lens (What invariant structures force the result?)
Normal functions, cycle class maps, and Abel-Jacobi pairings with holomorphic differential forms.

### 1.3 Counterfactual Lens (What if a key hypothesis is dropped?)
When geometric genus p_g > 0, Mumford's theorem shows CH_0 is infinitely generated, breaking classical finite representations.

### 1.4 Topological Lens (What is the space/bundle/cohomology structure?)
Intermediate Jacobians J^k(X) as complex tori C^g / (Z^(2g)); Griffiths bundles.

### 1.5 Geometric Lens (What shape, lattice, or metric invariants govern?)
Symplectic intersection pairing on middle cohomology; Hodge bundle curvature over moduli spaces.
