# HYBA product packaging and market framing

**Status:** Current owner-directed packaging standard. This document replaces older recommendations for multiple commercial markets or product families where they conflict with the direction below.

## Market-facing structure

HYBA has two market-facing companies:

1. **HYBA Data** — Chronus is a HYBA Data product/platform, not a separate company.
2. **HYBA Security** — AL HARAS is HYBA Security.

Do not present other pillars or capabilities as separate market-facing companies. Andre Taylor-Morris is HYBA’s Founder and CEO.

## Product boundaries

- Package client-facing products and services under HYBA Data or HYBA Security, with a specific, documented scope and owner approval.
- Quantum is a capability that may support an offering; it is not a standalone market, company, or product category.
- Bitcoin mining is an in-house treasury activity. It is not a customer-facing product, service, or market proposition.
- Do not create additional market segments or named product families—such as separate Hyperscale, Sovereign, Enterprise, Quantum, Intelligence, or Finance businesses—without explicit owner direction.
- A capability, prototype, adapter, or source-code component is not by itself a qualified commercial product. State maturity, limits, dependencies, and evidence accurately.

## Product quality standard

Products must meet a high bar for strategic clarity, usability, implementation quality, integration, governance, reliability, and operational finish. The internal shorthand is a “McKinsey–Accenture joint-venture standard”; this is a design aspiration only and must never be represented as an actual relationship, endorsement, or joint venture.

Emulate **Stripe, Revolut, NVIDIA, and Google** as quality benchmarks for finished products—their level of clarity, polish, usability, consistency, reliability, and execution. These are companies to emulate for product quality, not target-audience definitions or claims that they are HYBA customers, partners, or endorsers. Do not use their names or marks in a way that suggests a relationship.

Apply the [HYBA product style guide](PRODUCT_STYLE_GUIDE.md) to interfaces, demos, product documentation, and client-facing materials.

## Required content for packaging a product

Before presenting a commercial offer, document:

- Which company owns it: HYBA Data or HYBA Security.
- The intended user and specific problem addressed.
- The product scope, interfaces, dependencies, and supported deployment model.
- What is a capability versus what is implemented and available.
- Evidence for any performance, security, reliability, compliance, customer, or financial claim.
- Known limitations, operating responsibilities, and support boundaries.

Do not invent market size, pricing, savings, customer traction, certifications, or production status. Follow the repository’s [claim audit](../claim-audit.md) and [release decision](../../RELEASE_DECISION_MCKINSEY_TECH.md).
