# Receipt-derived revenue accounting — draft for accountant review

This candidate does not choose the entity's statutory reporting or tax basis. HYBA's applicable reporting framework, functional currency and local tax obligations require qualified external review; cash movements are not a tax return. This policy is an ASC 606 reference mapping, not an asserted audit opinion or automatic IFRS/DIFC determination. No pilot invoicing is authorised by this memo.

## Step 1 — Identify contract (ASC 606-10-25-1)
Admit the signed enforceable rights, payment terms, commercial substance and collectibility approval. Freeze the rider, period, currency and tenant/tranche identifiers. A contract digest alone does not prove enforceability.

## Step 2 — Performance obligations (606-10-25-19 through 25-22)
Evaluate platform access, implementation services and savings-linked consideration against the distinct and separately identifiable criteria. The variable fee is a pricing feature and is not automatically a separate obligation. Record the contract-specific conclusion.

## Step 3 — Transaction price (606-10-32-5 through 32-13)
Fixed consideration and constrained variable consideration, including the index-linked fee. The software profile requires tranche acceptance, elapsed dispute window and fixed vintage before its variable recognition event. This conservative software rule does not establish the accounting-standard constraint by itself. Assess probability of significant reversal and update estimates each reporting period.

## Step 4 — Allocate (606-10-32-28 through 32-35)
Determine relative standalone selling prices with evidence; document any cost-plus-margin estimation under 606-10-32-34 and applicable variable-consideration allocation exceptions. No unsupported allocation is booked.

## Step 5 — Recognise (606-10-25-23 through 25-30)
Assess transfer of control for each obligation; recognise access over time where applicable, other services over time or at a point in time according to the facts. Acceptance is evidence, not automatically the recognition criterion. This implementation covers only the stated savings-linked tranche profile, not subscription allocation or a general ledger.

Invoice before recognition: Dr Receivable / Cr DeferredRevenue. Acceptance against invoice: Dr DeferredRevenue / Cr Revenue. Recognition before invoice: Dr ContractAsset / Cr Revenue; subsequent invoice reclassifies to Receivable. Collections: Dr Cash / Cr Receivable. Advance: Dr Cash / Cr CustomerAdvances; apply against invoice or recognised performance without double booking. Admitted grid credit: Dr ContraRevenue / Cr Receivable. Each posting binds tenant, tranche, currency, measurement/event digests, index vintage, date and option. Cash movements are filtered independently from postings, not a second invented ledger.

External review and adoption remain outstanding. Determine applicable standards, entity-specific treatment of contract assets/liabilities and unconditional receivables, FX (including possible JPY exposure), impairment, refunds, taxes and presentation. Unit economics must use actual closed-period cost and revenue evidence, not test receipts. No statutory or investor certification is awarded.
