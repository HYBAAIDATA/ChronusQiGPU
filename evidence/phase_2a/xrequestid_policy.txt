# X-Request-Id sanity policy
# executed: 2026-10-06T17:51:54Z @ 7912c392f0e8e5cb4c7a92dcd5c43d11e436c1c5

Definition (chronus/mla1517_platform/python/control_api.py, at _REQUEST_ID_RE):
  sane = 1-128 chars, ASCII-alphanumeric leading character, then alphanumerics
        or any of . _ : @ - ; nothing else.
  regex: [A-Za-z0-9][A-Za-z0-9._:@-]{0,127}

Policy: every HTTP response carries X-Request-Id. A caller-supplied
X-Request-Id/X-Correlation-Id is echoed only when it fullmatches the sane
pattern; a malformed value is never echoed — a fresh 32-hex id is minted.
Denials are not exempt.

Enforcement: test_correlation_envelope (echo + mint),
test_correlation_envelope_rejects_malformed_ids in
tests/test_production_api_contract.py — sends malformed ids over the wire
('not sane!', 'has space', '(paren)', 'semi;colon') and asserts a minted
32-hex response, never an echo.

over the wire. No remaining hardening item named.
