# Chronus Workflow 08 — Weekly Operational Risk Review

## Weekly review

Compare connected GitHub and Notion against the prior review and this manual.

## Report

Material PRs/commits; architecture/authority drift; production-readiness movement; claim promotions/demotions; test/CI regressions; security and release risks; new/closed evidence gates; unauthorized canonical changes; packaging/deployment changes; workload/backend status.

## Risk fields

Severity · subsystem · change/risk · evidence · impact · recommended action · owner approval required? · status.

## Rule

The weekly review may recommend and log changes; it may not silently amend this manual or other owner-controlled canonical records.
