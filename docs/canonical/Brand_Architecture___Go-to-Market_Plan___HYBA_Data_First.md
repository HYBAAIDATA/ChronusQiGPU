## 2. Why the current logo is wrong (creative director diagnosis)

| Failure | What we did | Why it fails |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Wrong company orthography** | `DATA` as spaced all-caps gold display | Master logo uses a **company line under HYBA**, not a second display brand. User intent: **HYBA Data** — a company name, not a poster word |
| **Breaks family system** | One-off “DATA” treatment | Cannot cleanly produce HYBA Analytics / Intelligence / Finance with the same rules |
| **Hierarchy inversion** | Gold product line competes with HYBA | Navy carries institution; gold is accent — not a second logo |
| **Typography mismatch** | Thinner Times + tracking on DATA | Must match **ANALYTICS** optical size, weight, tracking, and casing rules from the master lockup |
| **Tagline ownership** | Reused under Data without decision | Tagline is house-level (*Intelligent Solutions.*) — keep once, don’t invent a new line per company unless intentional |
| **Spin-out readiness** | Looks like a campaign lockup | Spin-out needs a **system**: same mark + HYBA + [Company] |

**Verdict:** Do not invent a new logo. **Systematise the existing one.**

## 3. Brand architecture (Pentagram system)

### 1. The HYBA Ecosystem

HYBA Software Ltd is the parent institution. Under it, the ecosystem expands into distinct companies, each serving a specific civilizational and commercial mandate:

- **HYBA Data:** The foundational infrastructure and CIaaS substrate (First to Market).
- **HYBA Analytics:** Platform BI and advanced data interpretation.
- **HYBA Intelligence:** The AGI / EUCLID layer and sovereign optimization.
- **HYBA Finance:** Financial physics, HFT infrastructure, and quantitative models.
- **HYBA Energy:** Clean energy optimization (Prometheus), energy grid routing.
- **HYBA Research:** Deep scientific exploration (Millennium Maths, Physics).
- **HYBA Foundation:** Philanthropy, Turing Scholarships, COBBLA, and humanitarian deployment.
- **HYBA Security (AL HARAS):** Unhackable topological security and defense infrastructure.

### 3.2 Visual system (fixed rules)

| Element | Spec |
| --- | --- |
| **Mark** | Official hex only — no redraw |
| **Wordmark** | **HYBA** — bold navy, master weight/size |
| **Company line** | Same face/optical size as **ANALYTICS** in master file |
| **Company line casing** | **Title case for multi-word companies; single-word companies match master:** prefer **Data**, **Intelligence**, **Finance**; **Analytics** already exists as caps in master — **standardise to one rule** (recommendation below) |
| **Colour of company line** | **Navy by default** (matches Analytics master). Optional **gold company line only for launch emphasis on Data**, never as permanent family rule |
| **Tagline** | *Intelligent Solutions.* — house-level, gold italic; omit on compact/app |
| **Clear space** | 1× height of H |
| **Minimum size** | Mark ≥ 24px digital; full lockup ≥ 120px wide |

### 3.3 Casing decision (recommend — decide once)

**Recommendation (Pentagram clarity):**

- **Lockup company line:** small caps or tracked caps consistent with master **ANALYTICS** → e.g. `DATA` / `ANALYTICS` / `INTELLIGENCE` / `FINANCE` **only if** we keep the master’s all-caps line.
- **Prose / legal / UI:** always **HYBA Data**, **HYBA Analytics**, etc.

**OR (cleaner family, slightly different from current Analytics file):**

- Lockup uses **Title Case** company line: `Data`, `Analytics`, `Intelligence`, `Finance` — then **update Analytics master** over time so the family matches.

**Partner recommendation:** Keep **master optical system** (caps company line under HYBA) for continuity with the existing Analytics logo file; use **“HYBA Data”** in all prose. That is the least-risk path for a first-to-market launch.

### 3.4 What NOT to do

- Do not put gold on **HYBA**
- Do not redesign the hex for each company
- Do not make HERMES or PULVINI the logo for the company (product logos can exist later, secondary)
- Do not use “HYBA DATA” as a single fused wordmark
- Do not invent a new tagline for Data in v1
