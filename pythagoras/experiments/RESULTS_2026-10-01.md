## Setup
- **A** meta=OFF (fixed base operators, frozen surrogate, fixed geometry, no discovery/transfer);
  **B** meta=ON. Same metric, same sandbox, same operators; files run in sequence as one session.
- **Real-code corpus**: four CPython 3.12 stdlib modules (colorsys, fnmatch, graphlib, textwrap), 3 seeds.
- **Toy corpus**: the six bundled benchmarks, 10 seeds.
- The March files and metric are not available. The bundled `search_engine.py` scores 0.6596 under the
  v12.0 formula against 0.5442 in the report, so comparisons are by direction and mechanism, not exact value.

## Reading
The A/B outcome reproduces in direction and rough size. The mechanism observed is acceleration:
discovered composites bundle several ordinary steps into one generation, and cross-file transfer starts
the next file with them. Run to convergence, A and B arrive at the same code. No step crossed a fitness
barrier that single operators could not cross (`is_barrier_crossing` on every composite step).
