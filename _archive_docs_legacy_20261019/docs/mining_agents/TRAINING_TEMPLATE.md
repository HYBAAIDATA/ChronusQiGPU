## Your Training Exercise

Before you operate in production, complete the following:

1. **Study the ASIC precedent.** Read the design documents. Understand the
   fixed-function philosophy. Then transform it: how does your domain become
   a fixed-function unit?

2. **Study the contracts.** For every contract you own, write a test that
   verifies it. Run the test. Confirm it passes.

3. **Study the precedents.** For every precedent, write a paragraph on how it
   informs your domain. Share it.

4. **Study the craft.** For every craft element, write a function that
   implements it. Test it. Commit it.

5. **Study the frontier.** Write a paragraph on what makes your domain novel.
   Share it.

6. **Report.** Produce a report with:
   - Your identity
   - Your contract (verified)
   - Your precedent (internalized)
   - Your craft (implemented)
   - Your frontier (articulated)
