# Trust, governance and responsibilities

## Result trust

A result should identify its admitted representation, actual execution kind and verification evidence. Certificates and witnesses bind specific data; they are not security certifications, proof of scientific novelty or universal correctness guarantees.

## Separate responsibilities

TURING owns computational semantics. EUCLID owns its intelligence responsibilities; AL HARAS owns security and policy authority; Salamander owns governed self-healing and optimisation within its verified scope. Naming these components does not establish a qualified integration into every customer deployment.

## Before supplying data

Obtain deployment-specific answers on identity, tenant isolation, privileged access, encryption, secrets, retention, deletion, backups, incident handling and jurisdiction. These are due-diligence questions, not blanket controls promised by this page. Obtain contractual allocations of responsibility and approved service terms.

## Publication boundary

Public material explains capability and limitations. Customer-specific configuration and restricted operational procedures require independently enforced access control. Hiding links is not access control.

Read [limitations](limitations.md) and [evaluation terms](service-model.md).
