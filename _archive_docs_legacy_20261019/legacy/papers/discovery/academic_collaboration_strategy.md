## Fields Medal Nomination Preparation

### Nomination Requirements

- Mathematician under 40 years old
- Outstanding achievement of lasting significance
- Recognition by international community
- Peer-reviewed publications in top venues
- Letters of support from leading mathematicians

### Nomination Strategy

1. Build strong publication record (3+ major papers)
2. Secure letters of support from 5+ leading mathematicians
3. Present at ICM 2026 (invited talk ideal)
4. Build international recognition through conferences
5. Establish collaboration network across institutions
6. Demonstrate lasting impact through citations and applications

## Risk Mitigation

### Academic Skepticism

- **Risk**: Mathematical community may question practical significance
- **Mitigation**: Emphasize theoretical rigor, seek endorsements from established mathematicians

### Competition

- **Risk**: Other researchers may publish similar results
- **Mitigation**: Rapid publication, establish priority through arXiv preprints

### Funding Challenges

- **Risk**: Grant applications may be rejected
- **Mitigation**: Diversify funding sources, focus on theoretical significance

### Timeline Pressure

- **Risk**: Fields Medal age limit (40 years)
- **Mitigation**: Accelerate publication timeline, focus on highest-impact work
