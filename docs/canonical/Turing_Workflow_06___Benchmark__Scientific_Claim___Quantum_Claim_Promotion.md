# Turing Workflow 06 — Benchmark, Scientific Claim & Quantum Claim Promotion

<aside>

Material Turing claim promotion/demotion requires explicit owner approval.

</aside>

## Benchmark integrity

Same mathematical problem; same oracle/circuit semantics; same precision/exactness; matched work and stopping criteria; full setup/conversion/checkpoint costs when material; raw artifacts preserved; repeated runs/statistics; negative variants retained.

## Required categorisation

Mathematical formalism · classical reference · classical quantum simulation · Turing VQC · quantum-inspired · hardware-accelerated VQC · physical QPU.

## Claim packet

Exact wording; claim class; source SHA; package digests; workload; circuit class/depth/qubits/non-Clifford/entanglement; representation; backend; raw results; quality/error; resource use; baseline; statistical analysis; falsification condition; red-team verdict; owner approval.

## Hard rules

No physical-QPU claim without physical-QPU evidence. No quantum speedup claim from naming, iteration count changes, oracle changes or classical accelerator speed. No logical-qubit headline without representation/resource conditions.
