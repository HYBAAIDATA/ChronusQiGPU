## Conclusion

Restriction reduces iterations *when the winner is inside the subset* (~|S|/2 vs ~N/2) at a hit rate ≈ coverage = 1-|ψ|^n. Unconditional cost — failed subset scan included — is *higher* than unrestricted whenever coverage < 1, because misses pay |S| plus a full rescan. On a memoryless oracle the subspace buys speed on hits only; the exponents for both curves are ≈1.0. That is the measured result, published as measured.
