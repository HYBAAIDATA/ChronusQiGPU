## Status vocabulary

- `PASS`: Exact tested scope passes; cite commit, command, artifact and environment.
- `FAIL`: Reproduced defect; attach minimal counterexample and reference expectation.
- `GATED`: Activity requires unavailable approval, environment or mandated evidence.
- `UNAVAILABLE`: Backend, sensor, device or external service not observed.
- `MISSING`: Required artifact not supplied.
- `NOT_TESTED`: No current exact-head execution, not synonymous with success.
- `REFUTED`: Measured evidence contradicts a specific claim.
