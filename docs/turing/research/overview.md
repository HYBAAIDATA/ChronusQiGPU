# Research and reproducibility

## Publication status

The repository contains citation metadata, a draft paper scaffold, experiment runners and analysis resources. This is not evidence of peer review, an accepted publication or priority of discovery. Refer to the actual approved release and archive when citing an implementation; do not invent a DOI.

## Reproduction record

Retain the exact reviewed source and clean/dirty state, resolved dependencies, platform/interpreter, input/configuration hashes, seeds, observables, raw outputs, exact coefficients, certificates/witnesses, analysis commands, failures and limitations. Generate derived tables from retained raw records.

Separate repeatable mathematical payloads from job IDs, timing and environment-specific metadata. Seeded sampling reproducibility is scoped to the tested environment; cross-version bitstream identity requires separate evidence.
