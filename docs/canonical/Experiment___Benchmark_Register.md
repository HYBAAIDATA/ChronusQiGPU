# Experiment & Benchmark Register

<aside>

**“Make Ineffable look like an abacus” is recorded here as a falsifiable benchmark objective—not as a present claim.** EUCLID must earn superiority through matched budgets, held-out tasks, external baselines, reproducible protocols, and independent review.

</aside>

## Experience-first benchmark — 18 July 2026

### Protocol

- 20 matched random seeds.
- No human demonstrations.
- Three conditions: EUCLID world-model learner, model-free Q learner, and random policy.
- Stationary bandit: 400 interactions per seed.
- Delayed chain: 120 episodes per seed.
- Additional declared-transfer probe.

### Preserved results

| Measure | EUCLID world model | Random | Difference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bandit final-window mean reward | 0.904 | 0.493 | +0.411 |
| Delayed-chain final success rate | 0.998333 | 0.201667 | +0.796667 |
| Declared transfer probe | Passed | Not applicable | Internal only |

The corresponding knowledge claim advanced only to **adversarially tested**. It remains below externally compared and provisionally trusted stages.

### What EUCLID has implemented from the useful suggestions

- direct learning from prediction–action–outcome experience;
- autonomous curriculum based on mastery, progress, uncertainty, and difficulty;
- learned world model and simulated planning;
- curiosity without relying on human demonstrations;
- declared transfer across related tasks;
- exact, reversible, hash-linked experience memory;
- integration with causal, counterfactual, failure, and meta-memory;
- governed claims and prohibition on self-certification;
- matched control conditions and preserved JSON evidence.

### What remains unproved

No Ineffable system, code, weights, endpoint, or public matched protocol was available for direct testing. Therefore:

- Ineffable was **not directly benchmarked**;
- EUCLID has **not established external superiority**;
- the benchmark is **internal and synthetic**;
- AGI or ASI was **not established**.

## Required external ladder

1. Freeze protocol, budgets, seeds, and source hashes.
2. Add long-horizon retention and catastrophic-forgetting tests.
3. Add causal and counterfactual intervention tasks.
4. Add mathematical discovery tasks with machine-checkable proofs.
5. Add embodied or simulator-grounded environments.
6. Compare against published RL, model-based RL, and theorem-proving baselines.
7. Obtain human-expert and independent-laboratory evaluation.
8. Repeat on held-out environments selected after code freeze.
9. Preserve all negative outcomes and compute costs.
10. Advance claims only through operator-approved external evidence.
