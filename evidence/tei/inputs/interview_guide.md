# Independent customer interview guide
Obtain written consent and scope before interview. Record source window and evidence digest for each answer.
- Benefits: Which invoice lines and operational activities changed, and how were they measured?
- Costs: What fees, migration, staffing, integration and ongoing operating costs actually occurred?
- Flexibility: Which new workloads became possible; what incremental investment would enable them?
- Risk: What outages, data integrity incidents, assumptions and uncertainty affect the result?
Ask customer finance to independently verify the counterfactual and approve authorised disclosure. Do not infer savings from technical eligibility.
