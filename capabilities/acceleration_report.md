## Refusal surface

No acceleration over a competitive equivalent-task baseline has been demonstrated in these records. Exact arithmetic avoids rounding on operations representable in the ring; it does not automatically improve running time, compression, fault tolerance or predictive accuracy. The bounded product-state tensor experiment does not establish drift on the comparison path, deeper contraction, or advantage for arbitrary entangled states. The codec expands the measured page sample and the existing prime codec expands its measured log sample. No generic search, Fourier, optimization, learning, security or government-compliance advantage is asserted. Directly enumerated outputs have input/output and coefficient-growth costs; comparisons must account for them.

## Completion blockers

Provide defined F/R data and verify the pentagon; fix or explicitly bound the multiplier and measure timing against a named target and constraints; obtain seven-class ALU evidence and actual throughput; establish an interacting same-input many-body workload and report the outcome even if it does not show an advantage; implement and measure an actual page store and failure recovery; validate full repository integration and sign/approve evidence. Customer and operational acceptance remain open. No result above removes these requirements.
