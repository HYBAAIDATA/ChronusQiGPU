## Construction rules

1. Keep the official hex mark unchanged.
2. **HYBA** always bold navy (or cream on dark).
3. **DATA** always thinner weight + letter-spacing + gold `#F2A900`.
4. Tagline *Intelligent Solutions.* optional; gold italic when present.
5. Clear space: ~1× height of the “H”.
6. Do not recolour the mark except for approved mono/dark inversions.

## Colour tokens

- Navy: `#001E46`
- Gold (DATA): `#F2A900`
- Gold tagline: `#FFBE00`
- Cream: `#F5F1ED`
- Dark canvas: `#0A0F1A`
